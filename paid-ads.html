What This Service Is

Paid ads put your business in front of people right now, instead of waiting for organic growth. We set up and manage Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads campaigns with clear goals, careful targeting and regular optimization so your budget is spent wisely.

What's Included

  • Goal and budget planning
  • Audience and keyword targeting
  • Ad design, copy and landing page guidance
  • Campaign setup on Meta and Google
  • Conversion and lead tracking
  • Weekly optimization and clear reports

How It Works

Set the goal

We decide what success means: calls, leads, messages or sales.

Build campaigns

We create the targeting, ad creatives and copy.

Launch & monitor

We start small and watch results closely.

Optimize

We pause what does not work and put more budget behind what does.

Who It Is For

  • Businesses launching a new product or offer
  • Brands that want leads or enquiries quickly
  • Owners who tried ads before and did not see results
  • Businesses with a clear monthly ad budget

Case Study

Sample case study

A coaching centre in Tiruppur that wanted more admission enquiries for a new batch.

The challenge

Earlier ads were boosted posts with no clear audience, goal or way to measure enquiries.

What we did

  • Created a lead campaign targeting parents and students in the local area
  • Designed simple ads with a clear offer and enquiry button
  • Tracked every enquiry and adjusted the targeting each week

The outcome

Spending is now tied to a measurable goal, so the centre can see which ads bring enquiries and which should be stopped.
